Step-by-step guides

Three guides every new user needs. Follow them once and you'll never get stuck.

📖 How to buy data

  1. Open Buy Data — no account needed.
  2. Select your network tab: MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo.
  3. Tap a bundle card (e.g. 10GB MTN).
  4. Enter the recipient number carefully and choose payment: Mobile Money or Wallet.
  5. Approve the MoMo prompt on your phone (or confirm wallet payment).
  6. Save your order ID and track it on the tracking page.
Prototype: payment prompts are simulated and auto-approved after ~3 seconds.

👛 How to deposit into your wallet

  1. Sign in to your account (deposits require an account).
  2. Open Deposit.
  3. Enter the amount (min GH₵1), your MoMo number and network.
  4. Approve the prompt — your wallet is credited instantly.
  5. Use the balance at checkout — no prompts on every order.

🔑 How to reset your password

  1. On the sign-in page, tap Forgot?
  2. Enter the email or phone on your account.
  3. You'll receive a reset link/OTP (SMS or email).
  4. Set a new password (min 6 characters) and sign in.
Prototype: the reset flow is not simulated — use OTP sign-in instead.
