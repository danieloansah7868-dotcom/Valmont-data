About us

A data bundle marketplace built for Ghanaians, by Ghanaians.

Valmont Data is the digital infrastructure subsidiary of Valmont Group of Companies, based in Accra, Ghana. We serve individuals, agents and resellers nationwide with MTN, Telecel and AirtelTigo data bundles — paid securely via Mobile Money or a Valmont Data wallet.

What we do

  • Non-expiry data bundles for MTN, Telecel and AirtelTigo — bought on the website or via WhatsApp
  • A wallet system — top up once and spend from balance, no MoMo prompt on every order
  • An agent / reseller program — wholesale pricing plus a personal storefront link
  • Order tracking & automatic refunds — every failed order is refunded to your wallet without asking
  • 24/7 operation — buy data any time, every day of the year

How it actually works

We work with multiple wholesale data providers for each of the three major networks. When you place an order, our system routes it through the provider currently offering the best balance of speed, success rate and price — and automatically fails over to a backup if anything goes wrong. Trust & transparency

  • Secure SSL — every connection is encrypted end-to-end
  • Licensed payment partners — all MoMo payments go through regulated Ghanaian processors; we never see your MoMo PIN
  • Automatic refunds — failed delivery = money back in your wallet, no arguments
  • Live delivery status — network conditions shown on every buy page before you pay
