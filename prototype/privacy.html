Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026 · Prototype document

1. Data we collect

Account data (name, email, phone number), order history, wallet transactions, and device/usage data needed to secure and improve the service.

2. Mobile Money

All Mobile Money payments are processed by licensed third-party payment partners. We never see or store your MoMo PIN, and we never store full payment credentials.

3. How we use data

To deliver bundles, process refunds, prevent fraud, provide support, and send order updates by SMS/WhatsApp. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Sharing

We share data only with: payment processors (to complete transactions), network providers (to fulfil orders), and authorities when required by law.

5. Storage & security

Data is stored with encrypted connections and access controls in Ghana and GDPR-compliant cloud regions. Order and wallet data is retained for at least 6 years for financial record-keeping.

6. Your rights

You may request a copy, correction or deletion of your personal data by contacting support@valmontdata.com. Deletion may be limited by financial record-keeping obligations.
