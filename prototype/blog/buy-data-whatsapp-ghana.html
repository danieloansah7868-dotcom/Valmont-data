Buy data on WhatsApp in Ghana: the complete walkthrough

By the Valmont Data team · Jul 10, 2026 · 4 min read

Prefer WhatsApp over websites? Valmont Data supports full ordering through our WhatsApp support line. Here's exactly how it works.

What to send

  1. Network — MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo
  2. Bundle size — e.g. "10GB"
  3. The number that should receive the data

What happens next

  • You get a payment prompt (MoMo) or a wallet-payment request
  • Once approved, you receive your order ID by message
  • Delivery status updates are sent to you automatically

Tips

  • Check the live queue status on the website before ordering if you're in a hurry
  • Keep your order ID — you'll need it for tracking
  • Our WhatsApp line is staffed 24/7, but deliveries are automated
