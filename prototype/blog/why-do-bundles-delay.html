Why does my data bundle sometimes delay?

By the Valmont Data team · Jul 28, 2026 · 4 min read

You paid at 12:23 pm. The order said "standard queue ≈ 4h". It landed at 4:24 pm. That's exactly what the notice promised — but we know it can still feel slow. Here's the honest engineering behind delays.

Bundles move through network queues, not magic

Every data bundle is delivered through the telecom network's own fulfilment systems. On busy days — month-end, public holidays, school reopenings — those queues fill up. Your order is one of thousands waiting in line, and the network processes them in batches.

Provider load varies hour to hour

We route through whichever wholesale provider currently scores best on speed, success and price. A provider that's instant at 9 am can be congested at 9 pm. Our routing engine responds in real time, but it can't create capacity that doesn't exist.

Why we show the wait before you pay

We display the current fast-lane and standard-queue estimates on every buy page — before you pay. If the network is congested, you'll see it and can decide. That's the transparency we promise, even when it costs us sales.

What never happens

  • We never hide delays from you after payment.
  • We never keep your money on a failed order — refunds are automatic and instant to your wallet.
  • We never process duplicate orders for the same number silently (the second one is rejected — so never order the same number twice).

What you can do to get faster delivery

  1. Order during off-peak hours (late evening to early morning) when queues are shortest.
  2. Use one order per number — duplicates waste a queue slot.
  3. If it's urgent, dial the network's own shortcode (e.g. *138# on MTN) and buy a standard bundle at full price.
