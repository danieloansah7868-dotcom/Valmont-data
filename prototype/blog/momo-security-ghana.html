Mobile Money security in Ghana: 10 rules to never lose money

By the Valmont Data team · Jul 15, 2026 · 5 min read

MoMo fraud in Ghana is rising — but almost every scam follows the same pattern. Memorise these ten rules and you'll be safe from 99% of them.

  1. Never share your MoMo PIN. Not with "agents", not with "network staff", not with anyone. No legitimate business ever asks for it.
  2. Verify the payee name on the prompt. Before approving any payment, check the name on your phone matches the business you're paying.
  3. Beware "top-up" links. Only approve prompts you initiated yourself.
  4. Never send money to "unlock" a delivery. Orders never require extra fees after payment.
  5. Don't accept "refund" calls asking for an OTP. We never call and ask for codes; refunds go back to your wallet automatically.
  6. Double-check phone numbers before paying for data. Wrong numbers are not refundable — that's the industry standard.
  7. Use official channels only. Buy from the real platform or store link — look for the padlock (https).
  8. Beware "agent" intermediaries. Pay the person you're buying from, on the platform, not a random middleman.
  9. Set a daily MoMo transaction limit via the network's shortcode if you're not a high-volume seller.
  10. Report fast. Call your network's MoMo line immediately and report to the police cyber unit.
