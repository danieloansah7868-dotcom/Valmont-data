How to become a data agent in Ghana (2026 guide)

By the Valmont Data team · Jul 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Data reselling is one of the most accessible small businesses in Ghana: no shop, no stock, no license — just a phone, a WhatsApp line and customers who need data every single day.

1. Understand the business

You buy bundles at wholesale/member prices and sell to your customers at your own price. The difference is your margin. A typical agent sells 10GB MTN (member price GH₵40.50) at GH₵45–50 — keeping GH₵4.50–9.50 per sale. Sell 10 bundles a day and that's a serious side income.

2. Open your store

With Valmont Data you get a personal storefront: your name, your tagline, your markup. Customers open your link, pay you via MoMo or your wallet, and the data delivers automatically to the number they enter. You don't touch their phone, and you don't sit behind a desk.

3. Build a customer base

  • Start with your network — family, church, schoolmates, colleagues
  • Use WhatsApp status — post daily prices with your store link
  • Focus on non-expiry — students and market sellers love bundles that never expire
  • Be the reliability — deliver what you promise and customers bring friends

4. Grow to bulk

Once you have steady demand, use the Valmont Data API (or the bulk order form) to place up to 200 orders at once. Dealers and wholesalers get even lower tiers — the more you sell, the better your prices, the bigger your margins.

5. Rules that keep you safe

  1. Always verify the recipient's number before taking payment — wrong numbers are not refundable.
  2. Never order the same number twice at the same time — one order gets rejected.
  3. Don't advertise the platform on social media — our reseller policy keeps distribution private; your store link is your advertisement.
