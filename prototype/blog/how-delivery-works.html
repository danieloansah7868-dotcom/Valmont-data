Inside Valmont Data

How Valmont Data delivers data fast — the full technical breakdown

By the Valmont Data team · Aug 2, 2026 · 5 min read

Customers often ask us one question: "How is your data cheaper and still delivered?" The answer is the routing engine underneath the Valmont Data platform — the same engine this prototype is designed to mirror.

1. We work with multiple wholesale providers per network

For each of the three major Ghana networks — MTN, Telecel and AirtelTigo — we maintain contracts with several licensed wholesale data providers. No single provider is fast all the time. Network load, provider capacity and time of day all shift which one performs best.

2. Smart routing picks the best provider per order

When you place an order, our system scores every active provider on three live signals:

  • Speed — rolling average delivery time over the last 30 minutes
  • Success rate — percentage of orders delivered on the first attempt
  • Price — current wholesale cost at your reseller tier

The best-scoring provider receives your order. That's why you sometimes see a "fast lane ≈ 1h 52m" notice on the buy page — that number is computed from real, recent queue data, shown before you pay.

3. Automatic failover

If a provider stalls or rejects an order, the engine automatically retries through the next-best provider — without you doing anything. Only if every provider fails does the order flip to failed, and our system instantly refunds the amount to your wallet. No arguments, no waiting.

4. The delivery pipeline

Every order moves through the same pipeline:

  1. Placed — payment confirmed via MoMo or wallet
  2. Queued — routed to the winning provider's queue
  3. Delivered — the network credits the recipient number
  4. Verified — a result checker confirms the bundle landed; otherwise the refund path starts automatically

5. Why "instant" isn't always true

Truly instant bundles exist, but they cost more and are limited. Our non-expiry bundles travel through the cheapest available queues, which means waits of minutes to a few hours depending on load. We'd rather show you the truth before you pay than surprise you after.
