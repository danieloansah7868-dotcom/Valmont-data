2. Orders & delivery

Data bundles are delivered to the recipient phone number via our wholesale supplier network. Most deliveries complete within minutes, but delivery time depends on network conditions and is not guaranteed.

You are responsible for entering the correct recipient number. Orders placed for wrong numbers are not refundable.

Do not place duplicate orders for the same number and bundle at the same time — duplicates are rejected by our idempotency system.

3. Payments & refunds

Payments are processed via Valmont-Pay (Mobile Money and card). We never see or store your MoMo PIN.

If an order fails to deliver after all retry attempts, the amount is automatically refunded to the original payment method. Refunded orders are marked in your order history.

Amount mismatches (payment received ≠ order amount) trigger an automatic refund.

4. Auto-reload

Auto-reload is an opt-in service that automatically re-purchases a bundle when your data runs low. Each auto-reload charge sends a MoMo prompt to your phone — you approve with your PIN. Nothing is charged without your approval.

You can pause or cancel auto-reload at any time from the Auto-reload page or your dashboard.
