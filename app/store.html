How the reseller programme works

You open a store, you name it, you choose a markup. Customers buy through your link and the order is fulfilled exactly like any other Valmont Data order — same networks, same live prices, same Mobile Money checkout, same automatic retry and refund if a delivery fails. When a bundle delivers, your margin is credited to your store balance, and you request a payout to your Mobile Money number whenever you want.

You do not buy stock in advance. There is no float for you to fund and no megabytes sitting on your books: your earnings are the markup on each delivered bundle. That is what makes it possible to start with nothing but a phone and an audience.

  1. Create the store — a name, a URL like valmontdata.com/s/your-store, an optional tagline, your markup percentage and the MoMo number we pay you on.
  2. Share the link — WhatsApp status, your shop counter, a printed QR code, your Instagram bio. Anything that reaches people who need data.
  3. They buy, you earn — every order through your link is tagged to your store. Your dashboard shows orders, revenue and the balance available to pay out.
  4. Change your markup any time — 0% to 100%, saved instantly. 10% on a GH₵10 bundle is GH₵1.00 to you per sale.

One store per customer account, and you need a free Valmont Data account to open one. What you sell is the whole catalogue — every MTN, Telecel and AirtelTigo bundle we list, including the non-expiry MTN range and the rollover bundles up to 100GB.

Reseller questions

Does it cost anything to open a store?

No. Creating a store is free — you need a free Valmont Data customer account, a store name and a Mobile Money number for payouts.

How do I actually earn?

You set a markup percentage. When a customer buys a bundle through your store link and it delivers, your margin is credited to your store balance. Example from the form above: 10% on a GH₵10 bundle is GH₵1.00 per sale.

Do I have to buy data in bulk first?

No. Orders through your store are fulfilled from Valmont Data's float, not yours. You never hold stock and you never pre-pay for megabytes.

When and how do I get paid?

Your earnings accumulate as a balance in the store dashboard, and you request a payout to the Mobile Money number you registered. The dashboard keeps the full earnings ledger so you can reconcile every sale.

What does my customer see?

Your store name, your tagline and the live catalogue with your markup applied, at valmontdata.com/s/your-slug. They pay with Mobile Money or card and get the same order tracking and SMS confirmation as any other customer.

What happens if a customer's delivery fails?

The order is retried automatically up to three times and refunded automatically if it still fails. Your margin is only credited on delivered bundles, so a failed order never costs you.

Can I change my store name or markup later?

Yes — both are editable from the store dashboard and save immediately. The store URL (your slug) is what people share, so keep that stable.
