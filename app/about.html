About us

A data bundle marketplace built for Ghanaians, by Ghanaians.

Valmont Data is the digital infrastructure subsidiary of Valmont Group of Companies, based in Accra, Ghana. We serve individuals and resellers nationwide with MTN, Telecel and AirtelTigo data bundles — paid securely via Mobile Money.

What we do

  • Non-expiry data bundles for MTN, Telecel and AirtelTigo — buy on the website, via WhatsApp, or through a reseller store
  • WhatsApp ordering — text "hi" to our WhatsApp number, pick a bundle, pay with MoMo, done
  • Auto-reload — opt in once and we top you up automatically when your data runs low (you approve every charge with your PIN)
  • Reseller program — open a free storefront, set your markup, earn on every sale
  • Automatic refunds — every failed order is refunded without asking
  • Referral credits — invite friends, earn GH₵2 when they buy

How delivery works

We work with wholesale data suppliers for each of the three major networks. When you place an order, our system routes it through the supplier currently offering the best balance of speed, success rate and price — and automatically retries if anything goes wrong (up to 3 attempts). Failed deliveries are refunded automatically.

Trust & transparency

  • Secure SSL — every connection is encrypted
  • Licensed payment partners — all MoMo payments go through Valmont-Pay; we never see your PIN
  • Automatic refunds — failed delivery = money back, no arguments
  • SMS confirmations — you get a text when your data delivers
  • Order tracking — check status anytime at valmontdata.com/status

Our community

Valmont Data is WhatsApp-first. Our WhatsApp channel is where price updates, delivery status and promos are shared. We don't run accounts on other social networks — if you see one claiming to be us, it isn't.
